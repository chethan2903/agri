AgriSense Monitoring Node Localized Point-Measurement Architecture

Each AgriSense node measures soil and microclimate conditions locally around its installation point—a single sensor node does not measure an entire acre. Deploying multiple distributed nodes captures spatial variation across representative sections of the field.

1. ESP32 Microcontroller

Reads local sensor channels, applies filtering, and transmits telemetry packets over Wi-Fi to the AgriSense Flask backend.

2. Capacitive Soil Moisture Sensor v1.2

Measures localized soil moisture immediately surrounding the inserted probe. Approximate sensing-section insertion depth is 8–10 cm (approximate and model-dependent).

3. DHT22 Temperature & Humidity Sensor

Measures ambient air temperature and relative humidity in the crop microclimate surrounding the node enclosure.

4. MQ-135 Gas Sensor

Produces analog gas-sensor response readings to monitor localized ambient air quality variations near the node.
